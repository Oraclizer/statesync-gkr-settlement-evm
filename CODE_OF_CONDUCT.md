# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free environment for contributors
and participants regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste,
color, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or other protected status.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, precise, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence rather than people.
- Disclose uncertainty and correct mistakes without retaliation.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, attribution, and intellectual-property rights.
- Avoid publishing security-sensitive details through public project channels.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, discrimination, or sexualized attention.
- Insults, personal attacks, trolling, or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Coercion, retaliation, impersonation, or knowingly false attribution.
- Encouraging unauthorized access, credential disclosure, or unsafe handling of
  wallets and production systems.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when a person is publicly
representing the project. It does not create an employment, service, response-time,
or legal safe-harbor commitment.

## Reporting and enforcement

For confidential conduct reports, email `security@oraclizer.io` with the subject
`[statesync-gkr-settlement-evm conduct]`. Include relevant links, dates, context,
and any immediate safety concern. Do not include unnecessary sensitive data.

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, reject contributions, or
refer matters to the appropriate platform or authority. Actions are based on
context, severity, pattern, and participant safety. No response time or specific
outcome is promised. A maintainer who is the subject of a report should not be
the sole decision-maker where an independent reviewer can reasonably be engaged.

This code is informed by the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1:
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
