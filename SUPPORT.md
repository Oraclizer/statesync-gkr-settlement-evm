# Support

## Current status

This repository is private and unaudited. A historical V1 Base Sepolia
Root/Settlement evidence deployment exists, but it is unsupported and
permanently EVIDENCE-ONLY / ABANDONED / NON-ACTIVATABLE. No supported or
activatable deployment or address exists. V2 remains a private, unaudited,
non-deployed candidate with local reference contracts and a frozen test vector.
There is no release, approved production principal, supported network, stable
API, or production service. No production support, maintenance period, uptime,
compatibility, or response-time commitment is offered.

## Where to ask

- Reproducible non-security defects: use
  [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Oraclizer/statesync-gkr-settlement-evm/issues)
  and the bug report form.
- Focused improvements: open an issue before a substantial Pull Request and
  follow [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- Suspected vulnerabilities: do not use a public issue; follow
  [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) and email `security@oraclizer.io`.

## What to include

Provide the exact commit or tag, operating system, Foundry and Solidity versions,
deterministic reproduction commands, expected behavior, observed behavior, and
sanitized output. Remove credentials, private keys, wallet material, private RPC
URLs, and private infrastructure details.

Questions about third-party forks, unofficial deployments, investment decisions,
legal or regulatory advice, lost assets, or unrelated networks are outside this
repository's support scope.
